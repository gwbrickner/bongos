## Plan
Design comes from the `architect` consult (2026-09-29; the full text is in the session, the
decisions are D-140..D-147 in DECISIONS.md as each lands). Key points: premultiplied ARGB32
`0xAARRGGBB`; float only for path/flatten/stroke geometry, integer raster/blend/blur/decoders;
exact-area cover/area AA in 16-row bands (24.8 fixed, 8192-edge cap); fixed 16-rect disjoint
damage region; A8 box blur; PNG (all types/depths, Adam7) and BMP (BI_RGB/BITFIELDS) decoders with
size limits and an accounting allocator; goldens are exact, live in `tests/data/gfx/ref/`, are
never auto-created, and decoder oracles come from Python-stdlib `gen.py` scripts. Headers are
included as `"compress/compress.h"` / `"gfx/gfx.h"` (`-Ilibs`). Shared files touched: appended
lines in `mk/host-tests.mk`, `mk/imgdiff.mk`, ARCHITECTURE §2/§19, new D rows at the end of the
table. `uapi/status.h` and STATUS.md are not touched (M2.6 lane).
